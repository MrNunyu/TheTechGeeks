History of the Internet

1. Internet Society

Website: Internet Society
Page: A Brief History of the Internet
Author: Barry M. Leiner, Vinton G. Cerf and contributing researchers
Date: Accessed 14 October 2026
Used for: Information about J.C.R. Licklider's early vision of interconnected computers in 1962, the development of ARPANET, its purpose of sharing computing resources and the four computers connected by the end of 1969.

2. CERN

Website: CERN
Page: The Birth of the Web
Author: CERN
Date: Accessed 14 October 2026
Used for: Information about Tim Berners-Lee inventing the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN, its purpose of sharing information and the difference between the internet and the World Wide Web.

3. Smithsonian Magazine

Website: Smithsonian Magazine
Page: What Was the First Thing Sold on the Internet?
Author: Maris Fessenden
Date: Accessed 14 October 2026
Used for: Information about early online shopping, the secure online purchase of Sting's album Ten Summoner's Tales in August 1994 and competing claims about the first online purchase.
