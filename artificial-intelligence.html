Artificial Intelligence

What is Artificial Intelligence?
Artificial Intellegence (AI) is a technology that allows computers to perform task that would normaly require human intelligence. AI systems are capable of analysing large amounts of data in short amounts of time, and are able of creating new content such as text, images, videos and audio. Examples of AI include ChatGPT, Gemini and Claud.

AI and Copyright
Most AI models can be trained using enormous amounts of infomation such as text, images and artwork that is colected from the internet. Some of this infomation is copyright, which has caused many issues because some artists and writers has not given premition for there work to be used as training data. This has lead at many debates about copyright, ownership and whether AI companies should get the rights from creaters to use there work.

Digital AI Traps
A few artists and reasearchers have designed tools designed to protect artwork from being used to train AI image generaters without permission. An example is Nightshade, which was developed by researchers at the Univercity of Chicago.

Nightshade makes minor changes in digital images that are difficult for humans to notice, but can confuse certain AI models during training. For example, if an image appears to show a dog, it might be interpreted differently by an AI model. If enough images are ultered with Nightshade that are used with training data, the AI could learn incorrect infomation and prodice inaccurate images.

AI and Your Digital Footprint
Your digital footprint is the infomation you leave behind when you are using the internet. AI tools can also affect your digital footprint because infomation may be stored or processed when you use AI by the service.

Depending on the AI service and its privacy settings, some information can be used to improve future AI models. This creates privacy concerns, especially when people share personal information to the AI. To protect your privacy, don't share personal or sensitive information such as passwords, home addresses or private documents with AI, until you understand how that information is stored.
